## 1. Why Reddit & Quora are #1 for B2B Growth in 2026
1. **Google SERP Dominance:** Google's "Discussions & Forums" feature consistently ranks Reddit and Quora threads in the top 3 results for comparison and buying queries (e.g., *"Interakt alternatives"*, *"Shopify inventory overselling fix"*).
2. **AI Model Training Data (GEO):** ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini cite Reddit discussions as primary sources for software recommendations. Winning Reddit means winning AI Search.
3. **High-Intent Buyers:** Business owners asking questions on these platforms are experiencing active pain and ready to buy.
